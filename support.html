What to include

  • Your Player ID and game version, shown in the game’s Settings, if available.
  • Your device model and operating system.
  • A short description of the issue, the level involved and an optional screenshot.
  • For a purchase issue, the store order number. Please hide unrelated personal information.

Never send your password, payment-card details or sign-in codes.

Progress and purchases

If you use cloud saves, let us know whether you were signed in when the issue occurred. Store payments and refund requests are also subject to the store’s own procedures.

Your data

To request deletion of a game profile or personal data, follow our Data Deletion instructions. For other privacy questions, contact the same support address or read our Privacy Policy.
