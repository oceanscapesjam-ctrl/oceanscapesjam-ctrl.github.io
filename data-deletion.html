Account & data deletion

Your data. Your choice.

You can request deletion of your Oceanscapes: Shredder Jam game profile and associated personal data, or ask us to remove specific information.

Send your request tooceanscapesjam@gmail.com

Subject: Oceanscapes data deletion request

How to request deletion

  1. Find your Player ID in the game’s Settings, if you still have access.
  2. Email the address above. Include the Player ID if available, your platform and whether you want to delete the entire game profile or only specific data.
  3. Send the message from an email address where we can reply. If you cannot open the game, describe your profile and we will help identify it.

We may ask for limited information to verify that the profile is yours. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or payment-card details. You do not need to reinstall the game or create a website account to make a request.

Open deletion email

This opens a draft in your email app. Review it and press Send there. Nothing is submitted automatically by this website.

What is deleted

A verified full-profile request covers identifiable game-profile information and associated cloud progress, inventory, event and leaderboard records that we control, as applicable. We also handle requests for specific support, analytics or advertising-related personal data and contact service providers where required. Once deleted, progress and virtual items may not be recoverable.

What may be retained

Some information may need to be kept for legal accounting obligations, fraud prevention, security or unresolved disputes. It is retained only for the relevant purpose and necessary period. Irreversibly anonymized statistics may remain. We will explain relevant exceptions and retention requirements in our response.

App stores and other providers may retain records for their own legal or service purposes. Deleting an Oceanscapes profile does not delete your Google account, Google Play purchase history or other unrelated accounts. You can also manage Play Games data through your Google account.

After you send a request

We will review your request, verify the profile where needed and reply within the period required by applicable law—normally within one month for European data-rights requests. If a lawful extension is needed, we will explain why. Deletion is handled by support; it does not happen simply by opening the email link.

Uninstalling the game alone does not delete all server or cloud records. A game reset is not a substitute for a data-deletion request. For more information, read the Privacy Policy.
