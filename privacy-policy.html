Our registration number is 16191249 and our registered address is Tornimae 5, Tallinn, 10145, Estonia. Contact game support and submit privacy requests at oceanscapesjam@gmail.com; corporate privacy enquiries can also be sent to company@zbs.games.

Information used by the game

  • Game profile and progress: a player or installation identifier, chosen display name and avatar, levels, scores, inventory, rewards, settings and saved progress. If you use Play Games sign-in or cloud saves, identifiers and saved-game information are used to connect your progress.
  • Usage and device information: app version, device and operating-system information, language, approximate country, IP address, session and engagement information, level outcomes and technical diagnostics.
  • Advertising and attribution: advertising or app-instance identifiers where permitted, ad impressions and interactions, ad revenue, campaign and referral information. This helps measure ads, understand how players find the game and, where permitted, show relevant ads.
  • Purchases: product, transaction or receipt identifiers, purchase status, amount and currency. Payments are processed by the app store; we do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Support: your email address and any information you choose to send us.

The information processed depends on the features you use, your device, your region and your privacy choices. We do not request access to your personal contacts, ordinary Google Drive files or precise GPS location.

Why we use it

We use data to operate and save your game, provide purchases and rewards, manage leaderboards and events, answer support requests, protect against fraud, diagnose problems and improve the game. We also measure advertising performance and campaign attribution. A leaderboard can display your chosen name, avatar, rank and score to other players; avoid putting personal information in your display name.

Where European or UK data-protection law applies, our legal bases are performance of the game service or your requested transaction, compliance with legal obligations, and legitimate interests in security, support and service improvement. We use consent for advertising identifiers, personalized advertising and other optional tracking where the law requires it. You can withdraw consent without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Service providers and advertising partners

The game integrates the following services. Providers may act on our instructions or, for their own services, as independent controllers:

  • Google: Firebase and Google Analytics for game services, usage and campaign measurement; Google Play Games for optional sign-in and saves; Google Play for billing; AdMob and Google’s consent tools for advertising and privacy choices.
  • AppLovin MAX and its advertising partners: ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and ad revenue reporting. The partners relevant to your region and choices are identified in the consent interface where available.
  • Meta: app-event and advertising-performance measurement, including purchase and ad-impression events where enabled and permitted.
  • ByteBrew: gameplay analytics, technical and monetization measurement.

We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect users or our rights, or in a business transfer subject to applicable data protections. We do not sell lists of players’ personal information. Certain advertising disclosures may be considered “sharing” or a “sale” under some US state laws; applicable opt-out rights can be exercised through privacy choices or by contacting us.

This website

This website does not use advertising pixels, analytics scripts, embedded social-media players or tracking cookies. Images and styles are served with the site. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process IP addresses and technical request logs to provide and secure hosting, as explained in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Following an external link or sending an email uses the relevant provider’s services and privacy policy. Email links open your email app; they do not submit information in the background.

Your choices and rights

Where available, open Settings → Privacy choices in the game to review advertising consent. You can also manage advertising identifiers and permissions in device settings. Declining personalized advertising does not remove all ads. Optional rewarded ads are started by you.

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal data, restrict or object to processing, or opt out of applicable advertising data sharing. Contact us using the address above. We may ask for proportionate information to locate and verify your game profile, but never for your password. You can complain to your local data-protection authority; in Estonia this is the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate. We do not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Retention, deletion and security

We keep identifiable information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including maintaining your progress and resolving support issues, or as required by law. Purchase and accounting records may need longer retention; limited security or dispute records may also be retained for those purposes. Retention depends on the record and applicable obligations.

See Data Deletion to request removal of your profile or selected data. Uninstalling the game does not necessarily remove cloud saves or service-provider records. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

Our providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on applicable safeguards such as adequacy decisions or approved contractual terms. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your data.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 or below a higher minimum age required locally. Minors who are permitted to use the service should do so with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Updates

We may update this policy as the game or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current version. We will provide additional notice or request consent when required.
